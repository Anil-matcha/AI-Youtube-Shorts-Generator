## 1. Executive Summary & Root Cause Analysis (Old vs New Channel)

### Why the Old Channel (`@CasualGamer-786`) Froze
- **Burst Dump Penalty:** 229 videos were mass-dumped. The YouTube Shorts algorithm uses an "Explore and Exploit" seed cohort system. When 10+ videos are uploaded in a day, YouTube splits the seed impressions, resulting in tiny impressions (0-10 per video) and zero statistical significance.
- **Swipe-Away Metric Collapse:** In 2025/2026, the #1 gatekeeper metric is **Viewed vs Swiped Away (>70%)**. If a seed cohort swiped away at >35%, the video is dead-stopped.
- **Geographic Mismatch:** Setting the channel to the United States while speaking Hindi created a severe mismatch in initial recommendation impressions.

### The New Channel Launch Formula
1. **Pacing / Anti-Freeze Cadence:** Exactly **1 or 2 Shorts per day** (never more).
2. **Optimal IST Windows:** 5:30 PM - 7:00 PM IST (Primary) & 12:30 PM - 1:45 PM IST (Secondary).
3. **Curiosity-Gap Mobile Titles:** All titles under 48 characters so the YouTube mobile UI never truncates the punchline with an ellipsis (`...`).
4. **Keyword Stacking (460-490 Chars):** Maximize semantic coverage for Google Search & YouTube Search shelf without hitting the 500-character ceiling.
5. **Related Video Funnel:** Using YouTube Studio's `Related video` feature to route Shorts viewers directly to the 1-hour Full Episode 1 walkthrough for exponential watch-hour growth.
